1. Data Validity, Categorical Constraints & Target Leakage Audit (Stage 1)

SCHEMA VALID Zero nulls (0), zero negative numeric entries (0), and zero exact duplicate rows across all 6,362,620 transactions.

  • Categorical Constraint Confirmed: 100% of all 8,213 fraud transactions occur exclusively in TRANSFER (4,097 frauds) and CASH_OUT (4,116 frauds). PAYMENT, CASH_IN, and DEBIT contain 0 frauds.
  • Naive isFlaggedFraud Rule Failure: Catches only 16 of 8,213 frauds (recall = 0.1948%).
  • The Ledger Bug Proven: In 49.56% of fraudulent transactions, newbalanceDest stays frozen at 0.0 despite receiving funds, compared to 0.06% in legitimate transactions.
  • Outlier Overlap with Fraud: Univariate IQR outliers on oldbalanceOrg capture 82.22% of all fraud cases, and multivariate IsolationForest (5% contamination) flags 29.12% of fraud cases.
